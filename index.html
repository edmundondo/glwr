From listing to investable parcel

Verification and planning guide

01

Confirm the land

Obtain the official plot number, owner or permit holder, boundaries and authority to sell or lease.

02

Confirm non-use

Record last planting and harvest dates; obtain a dated field inspection and local-authority confirmation.

03

Test water and soil

Verify permits, borehole yield or dam allocation, water quality, soil condition and equipment ownership.

04

Price rehabilitation

Get an irrigation engineer’s report and at least two itemised contractor quotations.

05

Secure the market

Match crops to seasonal water supply and obtain buyer terms before committing capital.

Summer

Wheat, onions, green beans and other irrigated horticulture.
